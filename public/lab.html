1 Turn a chat into a deal

Pick a sample chat (or paste your own). The AI reads it and writes the agreement: price, deadline and payment milestones.

Takes about 5 seconds.

2 Client pays each milestone · 3 Release it

You play both people. Milestones are paid one at a time, in order.

  • Pay with PayPal: the client puts money into a PayPal hold. The freelancer can see it's there, but can't touch it yet.
  • Approve work & release payment: the client is happy, so the money goes to the freelancer.
  • Cancel the hold: the deal is off. The client is never charged.
  • Refund: give back money that was already released.
