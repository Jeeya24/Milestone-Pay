Four steps to getting paid

  1. 1. You both approve the agreement

    Send the client a link. They approve it on their phone, with no charge yet.

  2. 2. The client funds the milestone

    They pay with PayPal, and Milestone Pay holds that milestone’s amount.

  3. 3. You deliver the work

    Upload your files and mark the milestone delivered. The client opens or downloads them.

  4. 4. The client approves and you get paid

    The held payment is released and sent to your PayPal. Then the client is asked to fund the next milestone.

Questions

About money, clients and the chats it reads.

Is the payment real money?

Not yet. This beta runs on PayPal’s sandbox, with test accounts and test money.

Does my client need a PayPal account?

Yes. They open your invite link and sign in with PayPal. The same link keeps working.

What if a milestone isn’t right?

The client asks for a revision with a note, or cancels the milestone and releases the hold.

Which chats can Milestone Pay read?

Pasted text from any chat app, a .txt chat export, or up to 10 screenshots of 20 MB each.

Who can see my project and payments?

Only you and the client who joined the project.
